## Encouraged — please do this

These uses widen reach and are the intended default. You do **not** need to ask first.

1. **Preferred credit** (README, docs, talks, about screens, and redistributed NOTICE):

   > Noticing Lens is the open thought operating system from [ApexForm Life](https://apexformlife.com).

2. **Nominative use** in articles, papers, podcasts, issue trackers, and compatibility notes: “based on Noticing Lens by ApexForm Life”, “the ApexForm Life thought OS”, “compatible with Noticing Lens”.

3. **Keep the lockup on unofficial copies** that are personal, internal, research, teaching, or clearly experimental. Familiar chrome is how the house brand becomes recognisable.

4. **Link the house.** A live link to [apexformlife.com](https://apexformlife.com) next to the credit (AFOS™, Paradigm, consulting, coaching).

5. **“Powered by ApexForm Life”** in a footer, with that same link, on community deployments that are **not** sold as a competing hosted suite.

If you mention the origin, use the preferred credit. Do not invent a generic name that hides ApexForm Life.

## Requires written permission

Ask first (GitHub issue titled **Brand partnership**) before:

- Naming a **competing hosted or SaaS product** ApexForm Life, Noticing Lens, AFOS, or Paradigm
- Using the rising-line lockup as **your product logo**
- Claiming official partnership, certification, “ApexForm Life approved”, or co-branded packaging
- Registering domains or social handles that look like the official company or products (for example a `noticinglens.cloud` or `apexformlife-app` presented as official)

## Competing commercial products

If you ship a paid hosted product of your own:

- Give it **your** product name and logo so buyers can tell you apart from ApexForm Life
- **Keep** the preferred credit and a link to [apexformlife.com](https://apexformlife.com) (Apache NOTICE preservation plus this policy)
- Do not copy AFOS™ or Paradigm positioning, pricing pages, or support channels as if you were ApexForm Life

Personal, research, classroom, and internal use does **not** need a visual rebrand.

## Why this is written this way

ApexForm Life open-sourced the engine so the decision process can be inspected and extended. Credibility for that process — and sales of AFOS™, Paradigm, and services — depends on the house name travelling with the work. A fork that strips every mention of ApexForm Life is a lost impression. A clone that *is* ApexForm Life in the buyer’s mind is fraud.

Credit widely. Impersonate never.
